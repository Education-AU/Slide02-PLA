Now this seems overwhelming! But let us look at it in steps


First of all the calculation is simple. 

- From old coordinates the new coordinates are calculated by some multiplication of numbers summed up.

But what are the numbers $a_{ij}$

- For a fixed $j$ it is simply the coordinates of the initial basis vector $\mathbf{e}_j$ in the target basis $\mathbf{f}_i$.
