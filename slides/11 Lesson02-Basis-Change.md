Now that we have established that several basis can be used on the same space, how do we calculate the coordinates of a vector in one basis if we have the coordinates in another basis?

Say we have two different basis in $\mathbb{R}^n$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{e}_1,\mathbf{e}_2 \dots ,\mathbf{e}_n\\
\mathbf{f}_1,\mathbf{f}_2 \dots ,\mathbf{f}_n
\end{aligned}
$$

And we know the coordinates of some vector $\mathbf{v}$ in the first basis. That is

$$
\mathbf{v}= c_1\mathbf{e}_1+c_2\mathbf{e}_2 \dots +c_n\mathbf{e}_n
$$
How can we calculate the coordinates $d_i$ in

$$
\mathbf{v}= d_1\mathbf{f}_1+d_2\mathbf{f}_2 \dots +d_n\mathbf{f}_n
$$
