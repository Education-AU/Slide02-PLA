It was very easy to construct the basis change matrix $B_{b\rightarrow e}$ from a general basis $\mathbf{b}_i$ to the standard basis, and likewise from $F_{f\rightarrow e}$ from $\mathbf{f}_i$ to the standard basis.


So, we have now simply construct

$$
F_{f\rightarrow e}^{-1}B_{b\rightarrow e}
$$

First we transform from the $\mathbf{b}_i$ coordinates to the standard coordinates and the transform from standard coordinates to $\mathbf{f}_i$ coordinates by the inverse of $F_{f\rightarrow e}$.


This of course forces us to calculate the inverse of a matrix but that can easily be done in calculational tools.
