We are now ready to define the **dimension** of a vector space

<h3 class="h3-blue">Vector Space Dimension</h3>

1. If a vector space has a basis that is **infinite**: It is said to be infinite dimensional
2. If a vector space has a **finite** basis it is finite dimensional and the dimension is the size of the basis.

At this point two theorems should be observed


1. **Any vector space has a basis**
2. **Any two basis of a vector space have the same size**
