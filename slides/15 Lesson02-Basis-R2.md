So, we need the initial basis expressed in the target basis
$$
\begin{aligned} (1,0)&=a (1,1)+b (-1,1)\\ (0,1)&=c (1,1)+d (-1,1)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
a-b=1, a+b=0\\
c-d=0, c+d=1
\end{aligned}
$$
producing
$$
\begin{aligned}
a=\frac{1}{2}, b=-\frac{1}{2}, c=\frac{1}{2}, d=\frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
That is
$$
a_{11}=\frac{1}{2}, a_{21}=-\frac{1}{2}, a_{12}=\frac{1}{2}, a_{22}=\frac{1}{2}
$$
