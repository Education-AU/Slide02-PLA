So to move from representation in initial coordinates $c_i$ in basis $\mathbf{e}_i$ to a representation in new
coordinates $d_i$ in basis $\mathbf{f}_i$ we have
$$
d_i=\sum_{j=1}^2 a_{ij}c_j
$$
that is
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_1=a_{11}c_1+a_{12}c_2=\frac{1}{2}c_1+\frac{1}{2}c_2\\
d_2=a_{21}c_1+a_{22}c_2=-\frac{1}{2}c_1+\frac{1}{2}c_2
\end{aligned}
$$
